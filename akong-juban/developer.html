SHORT DESCRIPTION OF PROJECT

AkongJuban, is primarily created as a way of promote one of the oldest and history-rich towns in Sorsogon, as well as a final project for Readings in Philippine History, which is creating materials to promote our hometown.

WHY A WEBPAGE?

As an IT student, I possess the skills and abilities to create one. One of the faster ways to promote things is through the internet, especially as nowadays, we rely on the web to give us information about topics, we wouldn't otherwise know about without it.

Additionally when researching what to put into this webpage, I find that there is a lack of centralized information materials about my hometown, if there ever was, it would be scarce and incomplete, which is why I opted to create a static webpage.
